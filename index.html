    Terms & Conditions
    Owner Agreement — Please read carefully

    1. Platform Usage

    • KiraaBook is a global rental management tool for property owners worldwide.
    • You agree to use this platform only for legitimate rental management.
    • Misuse for fraud, harassment, or illegal activity is strictly prohibited.

    2. Free Trial & Subscription

    • New owners receive a 30-day free trial limited to 3 tenants.
    • Each phone number / email is eligible for only ONE free trial — duplicate signups are blocked.
    • After the trial, you must purchase a Monthly or Annual plan to continue adding tenants and creating bills.
    • Refunds are not provided after activation unless mandated by applicable consumer law.

    3. Data Accuracy & Responsibility

    • You are responsible for the accuracy of all tenant data you enter.
    • You must obtain tenant consent before uploading their personal documents and only collect data permitted by your jurisdiction's data-protection laws (e.g. GDPR, CCPA, PDPA, DPDP, etc.).
    • KiraaBook stores data securely via Firebase but is not liable for user-entered data.

    4. Tenant Rights

    • Tenants have the right to view their own data, bills, and payment history.
    • You must not use KiraaBook to harass, threaten, or unfairly discriminate against tenants.
    • All rent amounts and billing must comply with the tenancy and consumer laws of the country, state, or municipality where the property is located.

    5. Tenant Verification & Local Compliance

    • Many jurisdictions require background checks, residency registration, or police verification for tenants (e.g. India — Section 188 CrPC; Germany — Anmeldung; UAE — Ejari; etc.).
    • You are solely responsible for verifying and complying with the applicable laws in your jurisdiction.
    • KiraaBook provides storage for verification documents but does not guarantee or audit compliance.

    6. Payment Confirmations

    • Tenants can claim payment via the in-app "I Have Paid" button.
    • You must verify each claim independently before marking it approved.
    • KiraaBook does not process payments — money moves directly between you and your tenants via your chosen method (UPI, bank transfer, cash, card, etc.).

    7. Privacy & Security

    • Tenant personal data must not be shared with third parties without consent.
    • You are responsible for maintaining the security of your login credentials.
    • KiraaBook may suspend accounts that violate these terms.

    8. Notifications & Communication

    • WhatsApp/SMS rent reminders are sent from your own device. Carrier and messaging app fees may apply.
    • Browser/push notifications require your explicit permission and may be disabled at any time from device settings.

    9. Disclaimer & Jurisdiction

    • KiraaBook is a management tool — we are not responsible for disputes between owners and tenants.
    • Always maintain physical records as legal backup.
    • Any disputes arising from use of this platform will be governed by the laws of your country of residence.

    Tenant Agreement
    Please read before registering

    1. Data Consent

    • By registering, you consent to your personal information being stored securely on KiraaBook.
    • Your data (name, phone, email, ID, photos) will be visible to your property owner/landlord.
    • Your data will not be sold or shared with third parties outside legitimate property management.

    2. Local Verification & Compliance

    • Some jurisdictions require background checks, residency registration, or police verification for tenants (e.g. India — police verification under CrPC; Germany — Anmeldung; UAE — Ejari; etc.). Your owner will tell you what applies.
    • KiraaBook facilitates document upload for your landlord's records.
    • Failure to complete required verification may result in tenancy or legal consequences depending on your jurisdiction.

    3. Accurate Information

    • You must provide accurate and genuine personal information.
    • Submitting false or forged documents is a criminal offense in most jurisdictions.
    • Your registration is subject to owner approval after verification.

    4. Payment Responsibility

    • You are responsible for timely payment of rent as agreed with your owner.
    • The "I Have Paid" feature notifies your owner — rent is only marked paid after owner approval.
    • KiraaBook does not process or hold payments — money moves directly between you and your owner.

    5. Rent Reminders

    • KiraaBook may show you rent reminders in the portal and via WhatsApp links one day before due date and on the due date.
    • These reminders are informational and do not override your rental agreement.

    6. Platform Usage

    • Your KiraaBook account is personal — do not share your login credentials.
    • You may request data deletion by contacting your property owner.

    7. Vacating Notice

    • You agree to provide reasonable advance notice (as per your rental agreement) before vacating.
    • The vacating notice feature serves as digital notification only.
